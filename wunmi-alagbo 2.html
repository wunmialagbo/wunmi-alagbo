The shop

Three decades of herbal knowledge, one address in Ibeju-Lekki.

For over 30 years, Wunmi Alagbo has prepared fresh leaves, tree bark, and traditional agbo blends for the community around Bogije, Ibeju-Lekki. Every order is measured and mixed by hand, the way it's been done for generations — nothing pre-packaged, nothing guessed.

Whether you know exactly what you need or you're not sure where to start, a WhatsApp message is the fastest way to reach her directly for guidance before you buy.

  • 01Hand-selected leaves and bark, sourced and prepared fresh — not stockpiled for months.
  • 02Blends mixed to order based on what you tell her you need.
  • 03Reachable directly on WhatsApp for consultation, with home delivery and in-person visits available.
